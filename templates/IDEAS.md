# Ideas

A personal capture list. Log fast, don't overthink it. Every idea has a **status**:

- `raw` — just captured, not thought through yet
- `thinking` — you're actively chewing on it
- `filed` — archived, out of your active view, but not deleted (see Filed below)

Filing an idea away isn't a rejection — it's how you stay focused day-to-day. Once in a
while, revisit the Filed section: if an idea's still worth it (maybe with context you've
picked up since), promote it to `shared-ideas/` in this repo so the team can see it.
